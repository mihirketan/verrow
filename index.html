THE QTY ENGINE

Sizing decisions happen once a month, on purpose.

Your position size is fixed for the month. At month-end, Verrow checks two things: did you recover from a drawdown, or did you simply hold steady. Either one can earn you a larger size next month — but never all at once.

new_qty = old_qty × (1 + recovered% × k)
k = 0.5 by default — a full recovery earns roughly a 15% increase, not a reset to full size.
